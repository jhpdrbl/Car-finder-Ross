    Warranties by brand (new-car and certified pre-owned)

    Each car on the Available tab also shows the coverage it has left and when each part is expected to end. How the end date is estimated: most warranties count from the day the car was first sold new (its "in-service date"). Listings don't show that, so it's assumed to be about April of the model year (a car can be up to ~9 months either side; ask the dealer for the exact date). A warranty also ends when the car reaches its mileage limit; that date assumes you drive about 12,000 miles a year. The earlier of the two is shown.

    Price = advertised price + dealer fees (estimated when the page didn't list them), before tax. Monthly = $8,000 down, 60 months at 5.34%, 9.75% tax, $400 gov't fees. MPG = combined (city/hwy below); "EPA" means the listing didn't show it, so it's the official rating from fueleconomy.gov; plug-in hybrids show gas-only MPG.
